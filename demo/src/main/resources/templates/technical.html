01 · News

News Radar

News scraper with LLM triage

Pulls market news from RSS feeds every 30 minutes, merges duplicates and maps them to tickers. An LLM triages each event and rates its impact per ticker – as JSON, validated with Pydantic; invalid answers are discarded and logged.

  • Runs locally on Ollama (my RTX 3060) or on Claude with prompt caching and a daily budget
  • Built-in evaluation: hit rate after 1, 5 and 20 trading days

IonTrader

Stock analysis tool with a statistical model

Analyses a stock from its own price history: bullish/bearish probability and risk from 1 day to 1 year – plus S&P 500 rank, fundamentals, backtest and a paper portfolio.

The honest result: for META and NVDA no direction is proven – and the tool shows that instead of hiding it.

03 · Decision

Swing Rule Engine

Rule engine with walk-forward backtest

Indicators – SMA 20/200, RSI, ATR, relative strength – feed a deterministic decider. No AI in the decision. It shows how close a stock is to a buy signal in %, sets a dynamic sell target from past signals and needs nothing but price data.

  • Walk-forward: rules fixed on training data, only the test period counts
  • Trading costs included in every result
  • Overfitting warning once too many variants are tested
“I build tools that stop me from fooling myself in a backtest.”

Project 02 · NLP

When does fine-tuning pay off?

DistilBERT · AG News · University NLP final project

Frozen, partial or full fine-tuning – which one wins depends on how much labelled data you have. I tested all three across five data budgets (250–4,000 examples) and three seeds: 45 controlled, resumable runs with leakage-free splits and a learning-rate sweep per strategy.

Result: partial fine-tuning wins from 250 to 1,000 examples, full fine-tuning only takes over from 2,000 – frozen never wins.
